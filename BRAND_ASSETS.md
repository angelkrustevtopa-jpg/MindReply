# MindReply Brand Assets

`public/assets/brand/mindreply-mark.svg` is the primary compact mark for product navigation, browser/app icons, and small-format repository surfaces. It should be displayed on dark or near-black backgrounds with generous clear space. Use the text wordmark **MindReply** in the UI rather than adding text into the SVG.

The mark uses charcoal `#0B1715`, mint `#A8F3D0`, and blue `#5BD4FF`. Do not recolour, stretch, rotate, or combine it with unapproved product claims.
